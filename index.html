为什么做这件事？ 重要任务不会交给一个疑似降智的 Codex 去做；这里的 SVG 只是低风险探针。额度没怎么用却反复收到 “Selected model is at capacity”，同时怀疑模型质量悄悄下降。官方没有可查记录，所以这个账号每 5 分钟公开留下一份体检档案：错误率暴露容量问题，耗时、token 用量和任务失败是降智的早期信号。
In October 2026 this account began seeing unexplained capacity refusals and suspected silent degradation. This page makes the evidence reproducible: the Raspberry Pi runs run_round.py, appends to stats.json, rebuilds this page, and publishes both branches. A separate subjective quality series is planned: Claude Code will score fixed benchmark answers with a versioned rubric. No subjective score is shown until those reviews exist.
